About SkillBridge

Bridging the gap between classroom theory and everyday real-world confidence.

Why We Built SkillBridge

Traditional schooling equips students with academic formulas and historical dates, yet leaves major gaps when it comes to practical adulting—like budgeting pocket money, spotting digital scams, cooking safely, managing burnout, or appreciating art and culture.

Created by a student team of five working alongside two mentors, SkillBridge turns these six essential life pillars into a structured, multi-month learning curriculum paired with real-world case studies and progressive quizzes.
