Why We Built SkillBridge

Traditional schooling equips students with academic formulas and historical dates, yet leaves major gaps when it comes to practical adulting—like budgeting pocket money, understanding taxes, cooking safely, managing burnout, or appreciating art and culture.

SkillBridge turns these six essential life pillars into a structured 6-month learning curriculum paired with 36 monthly real-world case studies and progressive mastery quizzes.
